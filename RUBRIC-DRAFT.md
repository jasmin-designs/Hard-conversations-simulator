# Skill Scoring Rubric: DRAFT (not in use yet)

*Draft for Jasmin to review and edit. When it's approved, the coach will score every skill against these descriptions instead of its own judgment.*

Each level is tied to a line in the manager toolkit (`public/toolkit.js`), so the toolkit, the coach, and the scores all teach the same behaviors. **2 and 4 mean "between those descriptions."**

## 1. Respectful tone
*Toolkit: "Open with your purpose, warmly."*
- **5:** Warm, professional, and clear about why you're meeting; stays calm even when the employee gets defensive.
- **3:** Polite, but the purpose is unclear, or the tone is uneven (overly casual, or a little impatient).
- **1:** Sarcastic, threatening, shaming, or so casual the employee can't tell it's a serious conversation.

## 2. Specific behavior and impact
*Toolkit: "Describe the specific behavior and its impact, without labels."*
- **5:** Names specific facts (dates, times, deadlines) **and** the impact on the team or the work, without labels.
- **3:** Names the behavior *or* the impact, but not both, or stays partly general ("a few times").
- **1:** Vague or labeling ("You're always late", "unreliable"), with no impact named.

## 3. Open questions and listening
*Toolkit: "Ask an open question, then listen."*
- **5:** Asks open, curious questions and builds on what the employee actually said.
- **3:** Asks questions, but they're closed or leading ("Is everything okay?"), or doesn't follow up on the answer.
- **1:** Asks no questions, interrogates, or ignores or talks over the answers.

## 4. Acknowledging emotions
*Toolkit: "Acknowledge feelings before moving to solutions."*
- **5:** Names or validates the feeling, and pauses before problem-solving.
- **3:** A brief acknowledgment, then rushes to solutions.
- **1:** Ignores, dismisses, or minimizes feelings ("Don't worry about it").

## 5. Staying on topic
*Toolkit: "Stay on this topic. Don't stack up unrelated issues."*
- **5:** Keeps to attendance and deadlines, and gently brings the conversation back when it drifts.
- **3:** Takes a brief detour or raises an extra issue, then returns.
- **1:** Piles on unrelated issues, or lets the conversation drift away entirely.

## 6. A clear, shared plan with follow-up
*Toolkit: "Build the plan together… offer options within policy… agree a specific follow-up."*
- **5:** Builds a specific plan *with* the employee, within policy, with the details stated consistently, a written summary, and a dated follow-up.
- **3:** There is a plan, but it's vague, one-sided, changes as it goes, or has no follow-up.
- **1:** No plan, or it threatens consequences or promises things outside policy.
