Hard Conversations Simulator

Manager practice · Performance feedback

Practice the conversation before it's real.

Talk with an AI employee who reacts the way people do: open when you're curious and specific, guarded when you're vague or accusatory. A coach reviews everything you say.

How it works

  1. Prepare (optional). Review the HR policies and the manager toolkit, as you would before a real conversation.
  2. Start the call. You're the manager, so you speak first. Use the mic or type.
  3. Jordan responds in character and reads replies aloud.
  4. Your coach reviews everything you say. See notes live, or save them all for the end.
  5. End the call for a debrief on what worked and what to try next.
