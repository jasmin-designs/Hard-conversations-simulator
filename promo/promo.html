Northwind Logistics Policy HR-210: Flexible Work Arrangements

Policy ownerHuman Resources
Applies toEmployees whose roles allow flexible work
Effective dateJanuary 1, 2024

2. Manager approval

2.1The employee's direct manager may approve: adjusted hours within core hours; up to two remote workdays per week; and temporary adjustments of up to four weeks.

2.2Managers must notify HR of any arrangement they approve within five business days.

3. Department head and HR approval

3.1Hours outside the core hours, more than two remote workdays, or adjustments longer than four weeks require department head and HR approval.
